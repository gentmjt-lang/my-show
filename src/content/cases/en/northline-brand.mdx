## Challenge

Tight timeline, incomplete assets. The real risk was looking busy while failing to say who Northline is. We needed a scannable story order with limited photography.

## Approach

1. **Story before decoration**: One job per viewport — identity, product, proof, action.  
2. **Design in the browser**: Skip surplus mid-fidelity comps; settle spacing live.  
3. **Motion budget**: Hero title entrance + slow image drift only; honor reduced motion.

## Solution

Static site; form via mailto / third-party placeholder. Type and palette drawn from the brand book — no generic template colors.

## Impact

On-time launch. Conversion is mocked; post-launch we would track submit rate and primary channels. Performance constrained by LCP and image weight.

## Learnings

- Brand sites cost decision speed, not component count.  
- Structured gray blocks beat fake stock when photos slip.  
- Next: case thumbnails and press module.
