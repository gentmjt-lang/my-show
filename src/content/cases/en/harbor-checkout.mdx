## Challenge

Harbor’s buyers are mid-market procurement leads. Address, tax ID, seats, invoicing, and payment lived on five separate screens; mobile drop-off was worst. Worth solving because churn clustered after plan selection — acquisition itself was healthy.

## Approach

1. **Merge vs wizard**: Keep steps with a progress bar, or merge screens. We merged — procurement is a verification task, not exploration.  
2. **Persistent summary**: Seat and amount changes stay visible so buyers don’t backtrack.  
3. **Validate early**: Tax and invoice fields check on blur, not only on submit.

## Solution

Deliverables: new IA, critical component states (loading / validation / paying), and frontend implementation against the specs. The payment gateway stayed; only orchestration changed.

## Impact

Mock metrics four weeks post-launch showed +18% completion. Without an A/B harness we would still track checkout→paid funnel and session length, plus support tickets about “missing invoice fields.”

## Learnings

- B2B trust comes from a clear summary, not more steps.  
- Pull legal in earlier when compliance fields conflict with UX.  
- Next: a bottom-sheet summary on mobile so the form stays usable.
