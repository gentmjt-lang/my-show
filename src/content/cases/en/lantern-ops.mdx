## Challenge

Tickets lived in sheets and group chat; handoffs were verbal. The job wasn’t “another CRM” — it was productizing the shift: who owns it, where it’s stuck, when to escalate.

## Approach

1. **Narrow domain**: Tickets, queues, SLAs only — no analytics suite in v1.  
2. **Claim over assign**: Night staffing floats; claiming fits better than static assignment.  
3. **Light audit**: Status transitions only, not full replay.

## Solution

Static frontend + Worker API + KV/D1 placeholder storage. Dense lists first; detail drawer for notes and timeline.

## Impact

Mock comparison showed −35% handle time. Real metrics: touches per ticket, breach rate, and whether “can’t find context” complaints drop at handoff.

## Learnings

- Internal tools win on shift smoothness, not polish.  
- Analytics too early kills 0→1.  
- Next: keyboard shortcuts and bulk actions.
