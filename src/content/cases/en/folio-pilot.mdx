## Challenge

A hiring résumé site does not serve inbound clients — they need problem, scope, and outcomes. We also needed no CMS overhead and a simple Cloudflare deploy path.

## Approach

1. **Code as content**: Content Collections + Zod; missing fields fail the build.  
2. **Prefixed default locale**: Symmetric `/zh` and `/en` — no implicit root locale.  
3. **Design constraints in the brief**: Hero budget; ban generic AI template palettes.

## Solution

Astro static → `dist/` → Wrangler `assets.directory`. UI strings in `src/i18n`; cases in `src/content/cases/{zh,en}`.

## Impact

Success means previewable, mock-swappable, deployable. After real cases replace mocks: OG images and analytics.

## Learnings

- Narrative structure closes conversations more than visual skins.  
- Contact placeholders must be obvious so launch does not forget them.  
- Next: real covers, next-case links, light Work filters.
